What is a Panama schedule?

The Panama schedule is a rotating shift pattern that repeats every 28 days. It is built on a 14-day rhythm: two days on, two days off, three days on, two days off, two days on, three days off. One crew works days while the other works nights, and the two crews swap every 14 days — which is why the full cycle takes four weeks instead of two.

With 12-hour shifts the pattern gives you 7 shifts every 14 days, or 14 shifts and 168 hours every 28 days — an average of 42 hours per week. With 8-hour shifts the same rhythm produces 112 hours per 28 days, or 28 hours per week. Because the cycle never lines up with a Monday-to-Friday week, you will work some weekends and be free on some weekdays; the calendar above counts those weekend shifts for you.

The 14-day pattern

2 on → 2 off → 3 on → 2 off → 2 on → 3 off, then repeat with the opposite shift.

Panama vs 2-2-3 vs 4 on 4 off

  • 2-2-3 uses the same on/off rhythm as Panama. Many workplaces run it without flipping between days and nights — choose Day shifts only or Night shifts only above to use this page as a straight 2-2-3 calendar.
  • Panama adds the day/night swap every 14 days, which is what most 24/7 operations use.
  • 4 on 4 off is a different, shorter-repeat pattern with 12-hour shifts; it is not covered by this calculator.

Who uses a Panama schedule

Nurses and hospital staff, police and security officers, firefighters and EMS crews, factory and plant operators, and warehouse teams working 24/7 coverage. The appeal is simple: fourteen full days off every four weeks, and a predictable rotation you can plan a whole year around.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours do you work on a Panama schedule?

With 12-hour shifts you work 7 shifts per 14 days, so 14 shifts and 168 hours per 28-day cycle — about 42 hours per week on average. With 8-hour shifts it is 112 hours per cycle, about 28 hours per week.

Is the Panama schedule the same as 2-2-3?

The on/off rhythm is identical: 2 on, 2 off, 3 on, 2 off, 2 on, 3 off. The difference is that Panama rotates crews between day and night shifts every 14 days, while many 2-2-3 schedules keep people on the same shift.

How many days off do you get?

Seven days off in every 14-day half, so 14 days off per 28-day cycle. Because the cycle does not follow a Monday-to-Friday week, those days off fall on different weekdays each month.

Does the shift change every 14 days?

In the standard Panama rotation, yes: 14 days on days, then 14 days on nights, then back again. This calculator flips automatically. If your workplace does not rotate, switch rotation to day-only or night-only.

Why does my calendar not match my roster?

Almost always the start date. Some employers begin the pattern with two night shifts, and some use a different anchor week. Change the start date by a day at a time until today's badge matches the shift you are really working.

Can I import the schedule into Google Calendar?

Yes. Download the .ics file, then in Google Calendar open Settings → Import & export → Import, choose the file and pick the calendar you want. Apple Calendar and Outlook open the same file directly. Shift times default to 07:00–19:00 for days and 19:00–07:00 for nights; edit them in your calendar if your start times differ.
